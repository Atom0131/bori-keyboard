  `.bori/plan.md` and `.bori/handoff.md`.
- **At the end of every session:** add a detailed entry at the top of `.bori/session-log.md`.
  Cover:
  - The date and the agent that did the work.
  - Your decisions and the reasons for them.
  - Files changed, grouped by area.
  - Build results and errors, and how each was fixed.
  - Problems solved, known gaps, and what hasn't been tested on the phone.
  - The exact next step, and a short "How to catch up" list.
- Keep `.bori/handoff.md` short. It links to the log.
